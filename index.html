Pose With the Pros

An interactive fan photo experience at AT&T Stadium where fans step up to a kiosk and pose alongside full-body video captures of currentDallas Cowboys players. Powered by AT&T's 5G network, the activation repurposes concourse screens throughout the stadium, turning idle real estate into one of the most viral fan touchpoints in the NFL, generating massive organic social sharing from day one. Now in its fifth season, the experience has expanded from 8 to 16 screens, added a mobile "On The Go" version, and introduced cheerleader, mascot, and holiday overlays. Over 100,000 fans engaged with the experience during the 2023–2024 season alone.

My Role

  • Manage the on-site activation on gamedays, overseeing kiosk operations and fan flow across all 16 touchpoints
  • Coordinate directly with the technology vendor (Sector 5 Digital) on content updates, technical troubleshooting, and experience enhancements
  • Lead player capture shoots — directing Cowboysplayers through the full-body video capture process that powers the interactive experience
ClientAT&T × NCAA
Year2026
TypeExperiential / Content / Talent
VenueIndianapolis, IN & Phoenix, AZ

Men's & Women's Final Four

AT&T returned to the NCAA Men's Final Four with a high-energy, tech-forward basketball activation at Fan Fest in Indianapolis. The centerpiece was AT&T's LED basketball court — an immersive hoops experience where fans tested their skills while receiving real-time virtual coaching from sports commentator and former NBA player Kenny Smith. The footprint spanned two courts and included an AI-powered photo op that generated action shots of fans mid-play. AT&T also hosted meet-and-greet opportunities with basketball legends including John Calipari, Penny Hardaway, and Isaiah Thomas. At the Women's Final Four in Phoenix, the LED court returned with WNBA star Kiki Rice serving as the virtual coach, with appearances from Swin Cash, Candace Parker, and Jackie Young.

My Role

  • Concepted the LED court activation, developing the creative vision for the immersive basketball experience
  • Wrote scripts for talent, including the virtual coaching segments featuring Kenny Smith and Kiki Rice
  • Led the talent capture shoots on-site, directing athletes and personalities through their filmed segments
  • Managed the full on-site activation at Fan Fest, overseeing operations, staffing, and fan experience across both courts
  • Handled talent logistics for all celebrity and athlete appearances, coordinating schedules, arrivals, and on-site movements
  • Managed the activation emcee, ensuring energy and messaging stayed on-brand throughout each day
